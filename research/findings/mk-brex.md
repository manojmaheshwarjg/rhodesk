## 1. HEADLINE

Brex is no longer independent. Capital One Financial Corporation completed its acquisition of Brex Inc. on **April 7, 2026**. Brex now operates as **Brex LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Capital One, N.A.** (the national bank, not merely the holding company), Pedro Franceschi remains CEO, the brand is retained, and checking deposits still sit at Column N.A. (an unaffiliated bank) as of 2026-09-11. Brex's results are consolidated into Capital One's **Domestic Card** sub-segment effective Q2 2026.

### 2.3 The $5.15B vs $4.5B discrepancy (important)

The widely reported **$5.15 billion** headline is the announced deal value from 2026-01-22. The **$4,521M** in the 10-Q is the actual consideration delivered to shareholders at close. Trade press also disagrees on the announced cash leg:

| Source | Cash | Shares | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payments Dive (announcement) | $2.75B | 10.6M | 2026-01-22 |
| Yahoo Finance / StockTitan (close) | ~$2.56B | ~10.7M | Apr 2026 |
| **COF 10-Q (actual, primary)** | **$2.630B** | **10.6M ($1.891B FV)** | 2026-04-07 |

The $5.15B headline and the ~$5.6B "all-in" figure ($4.5B + $1.1B debt) are both in circulation. Some outlets (and Rho) describe **~$5.15B "including the debt payoff"**, which does not reconcile arithmetically with the 10-Q ($4.521B + $1.1B = $5.62B). Treat $5.15B as the announced transaction value, not a sum of the closing components. `[FLAG: recurring reconciliation error across secondary sources]`

### 2.4 Close date discrepancy

- Capital One newsroom and both 10-Qs: **April 7, 2026**.
- American Banker "Capital One closes Brex acquisition": **April 8, 2026**, and describes the deal as "$5 billion (half cash/half stock)" with "~35,000 business clients".
The SEC filing date (April 7) is authoritative.

**Brex Exclusive custom rewards:** companies with **over 50 Brex users** meeting minimum spend commitments can qualify for **custom uncapped flat-rate rebates** (negotiated with sales). **Tiered rewards**: contracted base rate accrues per expense, with a one-time points true-up grant at the end of the measurement period if the committed spend is met.

### 6.2 What a point is worth `[VERIFIED-PRIMARY - brex.com/support/redeem-brex-points]`

Brex's own help center, verbatim:
> "Most popular redemption options:
> Brex travel at a value of **1 point : 1 cent**.
> Cash or statement credit at a value of **1 point : .6 cent**."

Brex's own worked example (its page, not a critic's):
> "Say your monthly spend is $100,000 / 7x on rideshare at $5,000 / 4x on Brex travel at $25,000 / 3x on restaurants at $10,000 / 2x on recurring software at $10,000 / 1x on everything else at $50,000. You would earn **2,820,000 points annually**. If redeemed for Brex travel, your points are worth **$28,200**. You would earn a **credit card rebate of 2.35%** on your monthly spend."

That 2.35% is the **best-case, travel-redeemed** rate on a spend mix heavily weighted to bonus categories. The same 2,820,000 points redeemed for cash = **$16,920 = 1.41% effective**. On a flat 1x-only spend profile, cash redemption yields **0.6%**.

Other redemption data points:
- **Airline transfers:** "Regardless of alliance, **1,500 Brex points typically transfer to 1,000 miles or points**" (0.667 ratio). Partners: Emirates Skywards, Avianca LifeMiles, Singapore KrisFlyer, Air France-KLM Flying Blue, Aeromexico Club Premier, Cathay Asia Miles, Qantas Frequent Flyer (Oneworld / SkyTeam / Star Alliance).
- **Offsites:** initial fee **25,000 Brex points ($250)** → implies **1 cent/point** for that redemption; group must be >15 people, booked ≥2 months ahead.
- **Swag:** minimum **20,000 Brex points**.
