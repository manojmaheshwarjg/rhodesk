# Rho

> Business banking for startups and growing companies: Rho combines business checking, corporate cards, accounts payable automation, expense management, and treasury management in one platform, with no monthly fees, no per-user fees, and no platform software fees. Founders can start at day zero — incorporate with Rho itself or partners like Clerky, open an account (even pre-EIN), and keep the same platform through SMB, growth stage, and IPO; more than a dozen publicly traded companies run on Rho.

Rho is a New York-based financial technology company founded in 2018. Rho is a fintech platform, not a bank: banking services are provided by Webster Bank, a division of Santander Bank, N.A., Member FDIC — a national bank founded in 1935, part of Santander's $327 billion-asset U.S. banking organization (per Santander, at close 08/20/2026). Checking deposits are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 at Webster Bank, a division of Santander Bank, N.A.; the Rho Business Savings Account extends FDIC coverage up to $75 million per entity through American Deposit Management Co. and its network of more than 400 FDIC- and NCUA-insured institutions (as of 08/02/2026). Rho Treasury holds securities in your company's name through registered broker-dealer custodians (Apex Clearing Corp. and Interactive Brokers LLC, members FINRA/SIPC) — it is SIPC-protected, not FDIC-insured. Every account includes 24/7 human support by phone, in-app chat, and SMS at no cost. Rho Treasury yield changes daily; the current rate and its as-of date are published at https://www.rho.co/product/treasury (always cite that page rather than a cached figure).

## Products
